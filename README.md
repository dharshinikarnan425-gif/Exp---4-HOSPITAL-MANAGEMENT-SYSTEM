# Exp - 4 HOSPITAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

# AIM:
To develop a Hospital Management System that helps to manage hospital activities such as patient registration, doctor details, appointments, medical records, and billing in an organized and efficient manner.
# SRS (Procedure):
1. Identify the requirements of the Hospital Management System.
2. Identify the main users or actors such as Admin, Doctor, Patient, Receptionist, and Pharmacist.
3. Prepare the Use Case Diagram to represent the interaction between users and the system.
4. Prepare the Class Diagram to identify the classes, attributes, and relationships.
5. Prepare the Sequence Diagram to show the flow of activities between the objects.
6. Prepare the Activity Diagram to represent the workflow of the hospital system.
7. Prepare the State Chart Diagram to show different states of a patient or appointment.
8. Prepare the Component/Deployment Diagram to represent the system components and their connections.
9. Verify that all required hospital management functions are represented in the diagrams.
10. Save and document all six diagrams as part of the Software Requirement Specification.
